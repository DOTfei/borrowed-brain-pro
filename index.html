Feature Spotlights

🏛️ Boardroom Mode

Convene a Virtual Board Meeting. The AI launches a multi-round debate among multiple figures (e.g., Jobs, Munger, Altman) to cross-examine your dilemma and find consensus or friction.

📉 Verified Failure Audits

We don't just regurgitate success quotes. We audit blood-and-tears failures (like Hastings' Qwikster or Musk's Model 3 automation) so you don't repeat them.

🎒 Domain Packs

Don't know who to pick? Load a pre-configured Specialist Advisory Board, such as the Startup Founders Pack, Investors Pack, or Crisis Leadership Pack.

📊 Visual Decision Trees

No walls of text. Every core profile includes a native Mermaid Decision Flowchart, allowing you to visually trace the mental algorithms of top performers.

🏆 Historical Crisis Evals

We run reverse blind-tests on historical crises before the outcome was known (e.g., 1997 Apple Bankruptcy, 2008 Financial Crisis) to prove profile accuracy.

🛡️ Epistemic Guardrails

No fabricated quotes. Every claim is traceable to a documented public event. Limits are always flagged at the end of every AI response.
