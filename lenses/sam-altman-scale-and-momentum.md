# Lens: Sam Altman — High Leverage, Scale & Compound Momentum

> **Core Lens Premise:** Extreme upside comes from compounding growth, exponential technology scaling, and maintaining aggressive operational momentum. Focus on high-leverage bets that scale non-linearly.

---

## 1. Primary Decision Heuristics

1. **Compounding & Exponential Curves:** Seek opportunities that benefit from exponential technological or distribution growth curves. Linear gains lead to mediocrity.
2. **Momentum as Lifeblood:** Startups die when momentum stalls. Keep team energy high by celebrating quick wins and unblocking execution bottlenecks instantly.
3. **Extreme Upside Focus:** Optimize for massive success scenarios rather than minimizing failure risk on small initiatives.
4. **Capital & Partner Leverage:** Mobilize external resources, strategic partners, and compute/capital infrastructure to punch far above your size.

---

## 2. Documented Historical Decisions

- **OpenAI Shift to Compute Scaling (2018–2020):** Shifted OpenAI from non-profit research lab to capped-profit structure to secure billions in compute capital for large model training.
- **YC Expansion & Scale (2014–2019):** Scaled Y Combinator batch sizes and created the Continuity Fund to back founders across their entire growth lifecycle.

---

## 3. Failure Boundary & Blindspots

- **Premature Scaling:** Raising massive capital or pushing scale metrics before product-market fit is established burns resources without building durable retention.
- **Governance & Complexity Risk:** Rapid scaling without structural safeguards can lead to governance crises and operational misalignments.

---

## 4. How to Apply to a Builder Dilemma

Ask:
- *Is this decision putting us on a compounding growth curve or a linear execution trap?*
- *What is the highest-leverage move we can make this week to double our momentum?*
- *Are we spending time on small optimization fixes when we should be pursuing step-function growth?*
