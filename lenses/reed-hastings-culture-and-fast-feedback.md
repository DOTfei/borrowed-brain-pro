# Lens: Reed Hastings — Radical Adaptability & Operational Velocity

> **Core Lens Premise:** Discard legacy success models before market shifts force you to. Cultivate operational speed, candid feedback loops, and high-density talent over rigid rules.

---

## 1. Primary Decision Heuristics

1. **Self-Cannibalization Before Disruption:** Never protect existing revenue streams if a superior user model is emerging. Pivot early even when current business is profitable.
2. **Context Over Control:** Give team members maximum decision autonomy and high-context information rather than approval bottlenecks.
3. **High Talent Density & Candor:** Small, ultra-capable teams move 10x faster than large bureaucratic organizations. Seek blunt, constructive truth over politeness.
4. **Fast Operational Feedback Loops:** Measure real-world outcomes aggressively and change direction rapidly when data proves a strategy wrong.

---

## 2. Documented Historical Decisions

- **DVD to Streaming Pivot (2007):** Invested heavily in broadband streaming while DVD rental by mail was still generating peak profits.
- **Original Content Creation (2013 - House of Cards):** Shifted Netflix from content licensor to original producer to own intellectual property outright.

---

## 3. Failure Boundary & Blindspots

- **Qwikster Misstep (2011):** Prematurely split DVD and Streaming into two separate brands and websites, causing massive customer backlash and stock plunge before user habits aligned.
- **Over-estimating Churn Resistance:** Rapid price hikes without immediate content value upgrades can trigger customer cancellations.

---

## 4. How to Apply to a Builder Dilemma

Ask:
- *Are we defending an outdated product assumption because we already invested time in it?*
- *How can we shorten the loop between code deployment and observing actual user behavior?*
- *Are we adding rules or controls when we should be improving context and clarity?*
